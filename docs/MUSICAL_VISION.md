# Cosmophony — Musical Vision (the Composition Bible)

**Status: authoritative creative direction, set by Shambu + Atlas HQ (2026-08-05).**
The Living Sky design note (Slice A0) and every musical decision after it are
written AGAINST this document. Engineering realizes this vision; it does not
re-derive it. Read alongside `LIVING_SKY_HANDOFF.md` (the build contract) and
the ChatGPT brainstorm package's grammar ideas (perception / conductor / phrase
grammar), which this document absorbs and grounds in truth.

---

## 0. The one-sentence brief

Turn the true sky into music that *elevates* — Sigur Rós's cathartic builds,
Cigarettes After Sex's warmth, Beethoven's simplicity-with-depth, Zimmer's
few-notes-vast-scale, Kid A's electronic texture, and handpan's hours-long
calm — while every note remains a real star doing a real thing.

The emotional target, in Shambu's words: *a playground for self vs non-self* —
one small listener against, and inside, the all.

---

## 1. The reference palette, decoded into mechanisms

These six references are not vibes to imitate; each one names a concrete
compositional mechanism we implement. Together they form one coherent style —
which is lucky, and worth noticing: **all six share the same skeleton** (a
sustained foundation + slow additive growth + tiny amounts of melodic material
+ lots of space). That skeleton is exactly our architecture.

| Reference | The mechanism we take |
|---|---|
| **Sigur Rós** | The **long arc**: 8–12 minutes of patient build to one cathartic bloom, then release. Nothing is hurried; the climax is *earned*. |
| **Cigarettes After Sex** | **Warmth + smooth motion**: slow tempo band (~50–65 bpm feel), soft attacks, and chords that change by the *smallest possible step* (voice-leading; keep common tones) so harmony glides instead of jumping. |
| **Beethoven** | **Motif economy**: a tiny cell of 3–5 notes, developed relentlessly (repeated, transposed, inverted, stretched) instead of new material. Depth from ONE idea, not many. |
| **Hans Zimmer ("Time")** | The **additive engine**: one simple harmonic cycle repeating while each pass adds exactly one layer — then strips them away. It's an *algorithm*, and we implement it literally. Plus: deep low-end for scale. |
| **Radiohead (Kid A)** | **Texture as emotion**: analog warble, soft electronic pulse, unease that resolves. Licenses one mood to have a gentle *pulse* and synthetic colour. |
| **Handpan (hours-long)** | **Rhythm without percussion aggression**: soft cyclic figuration that hypnotizes rather than demands, and proof that repetition + tiny variation can be listenable for hours. |

## 2. The three breakthroughs (the heart of the design)

### 2.1 A constellation's SHAPE is its melody — identity born from truth

The tension we carried: fixed signature motifs (memorable) vs. truth (the notes
must come from the sky). It dissolves completely with one observation:

**A constellation's internal geometry never changes.** Orion's stars keep the
same pattern from every place on Earth, on every date, for a human lifetime.
So: derive each constellation's motif by *tracing its stick-figure* — its
brightest stars in a fixed canonical order (the asterism path), their relative
positions mapped to contour and rhythm, snapped to the scale as always.

- Orion's theme IS Orion's shape, sung. It is *the same recognizable contour*
  in Bengaluru, Tokyo, or Reykjavík — because the shape is the same.
- Identity is not authored; it is **discovered in the data**. Nothing is
  invented. "Learnable cosmic language" and "structure is true" become the
  same feature.
- Dose (per Shambu): a *little* signature — the motif appears when its
  constellation is prominent, developed Beethoven-style; it never dominates
  the grandiose whole.
- Shareable story for free: *"This is what Orion sounds like."*

### 2.2 The night is already a symphony — we just have to play the real arc

Stars rise, climb to their highest point (culmination), and set. Sky regions of
different richness rotate through. That IS build → peak → release; the
Sigur Rós arc and the Zimmer additive engine don't need to be faked — they need
to be **synchronized to the true night**:

- The **additive engine** runs on the real sky: as the night advances (at our
  chosen time-compression), rising stars ADD layers, setting stars REMOVE them.
- The **cathartic bloom** is scheduled at that night's *actual* most dramatic
  true event — the brightest star's culmination, or the richest star-field
  (Milky Way band) cresting the horizon. Every night has a different, real
  climax. The music swells because the sky genuinely does.
- **Development is true too**: a rising star's phrase ascends; a setting star's
  phrase is *inverted* — it descends, because the star is literally going
  down. Augmentation (slowing the motif) at culmination = the star's still
  moment at the top of its arc.
- Two session shapes from one engine:
  - **Birth-sky session (~8–15 min):** a composed arc — the night unfolds from
    your moment, blooms, releases. A piece with a beginning and an end.
  - **Endless mode:** the same engine free-running for all-day listening; the
    arc keeps breathing with the real (compressed) sky, never repeating
    exactly (generative layering below).

### 2.3 The sky composes its own emotion — "emotional weather"

Per Shambu: a lonely sky SHOULD sound lonely. The full range — wonder,
grandeur, melancholy, mystery, intimacy — belongs in the instrument. So the
scene's *true statistics* select the emotional weather, deterministically:

Measured per scene (all real): visible-star count, integrated brightness,
brightest-star magnitude, spatial spread/clustering, fraction of sky occupied
by rich fields.

- **Sparse + dim** (polar winter, light-polluted city) → *Lonely/introspective*:
  minor colour, one solo voice, long silences, small register. Honest, and
  beautiful for exactly the listeners Shambu named.
- **Rich + brilliant** (dark-site Milky Way) → *Grandiose*: full stack, wide
  registers, brighter modes, the works.
- Between: a continuous blend, not hard categories.

Two independent dials, clearly separated:
- **The sky chooses the weather** (density, mode colour, silence budget,
  register span) — truth.
- **The user chooses the lens** (instrument mood preset, §5) — taste.

## 3. The performance architecture — five simultaneous roles

Every sound in the piece belongs to exactly one role. (This absorbs the
ChatGPT package's role/conductor idea, grounded.)

1. **GROUND** — the drone/bass foundation (have it, from Phase 3/3.5). The
   root + deep low-end (Zimmer scale). Represents space itself. Always sounding,
   always breathing.
2. **CHORD** — the harmony bed: the currently-visible bright stars as sustained
   tones (today's engine). NEW: as the sky turns, the chord *changes* — with a
   **voice-leading rule**: prefer the revoicing that moves least (keep common
   tones), so harmony glides (Cigarettes After Sex motion).
3. **PULSE** — optional per mood: soft cyclic figuration (handpan / felt piano
   / Kid A synth pulse). Slow, gentle, hypnotic. Labeled honestly as artistic
   clothing (tempo is not an astronomical fact).
4. **LEAD — "the sky speaks."** Sparse melody driven by TRUE events only:
   - a notable star **rises** → a short announcing phrase (ascending)
   - a star **culminates** → its chime, the fullest statement (augmented)
   - a star **sets** → a farewell (inverted, descending, quieter)
   - a prominent **constellation** is up → its shape-motif (§2.1), developed
   Phrasing is composed, not random: **statement → answer → rest** (the
   ChatGPT phrase grammar), under a strict **note budget** — the Conductor
   picks at most one event-phrase per window, choosing the most *salient*
   (perception layer: brightest / most prominent wins).
5. **WEATHER** — texture and atmosphere: the 3.5 shimmer, swells tied to the
   arc, scene-level dynamics. Where Sigur Rós lives.

**Silence is a scheduled event.** Every scene has a silence budget (larger for
sparse skies). After each lead phrase, a rest proportional to the sky's
emptiness. Space is not the absence of the piece; it is part of it.

**Generative non-repetition** (Eno): GROUND breath, PULSE cycle, CHORD
revoicing, and WEATHER swell run on incommensurate cycle lengths, so the
combined texture never exactly recurs even before the sky moves.

## 4. The opening gesture — self vs non-self, made audible

Every birth-sky session opens the same way, and this is the emotional thesis
of the whole product:

**First: one star, alone.** The brightest star of YOUR sky plays its tone into
silence — small, single, particular. *That is you.*

**Then: the sky answers.** The remaining voices bloom in around it, brightness
order, over the first ~60–90 seconds (we already built blooming entries), until
your star is one voice inside the whole. *That is everything else — and you,
inside it.*

The session's closing mirrors it: the texture thins until the last voice
sounding is that same first star. One → all → one. This costs almost nothing
to implement and is the moment people will remember and share.

## 5. The mood lenses (instrument presets)

Five lenses, each an explicit homage to the reference palette. (Amends the
earlier 4-mood working set — same idea, now mapped to the references.) All use
free/openly-licensed samples per the handoff's sourcing slice; each lens states
its palette; switching lenses NEVER changes pitches/timing (tested — the
Phase 3.5 style-invariance test extends to moods).

| Lens | Homage | Palette (all free-licensed) |
|---|---|---|
| **Aurora** | Sigur Rós | bowed-string pads, distant choir, huge slow swells, brightest modes |
| **Embrace** | Cigarettes After Sex | felt piano, warm pads, vibraphone glow, closest & most intimate |
| **Sonata** | Beethoven | felt piano + chamber strings, motif-forward, the most "composed" lens |
| **Pulse** | Kid A | analog-style synths, soft pulse, tape warble, the electronic lens |
| **Ground** | handpan hours | handpan, singing bowls, deep drone — the endless-background lens |

Default: **Aurora** for birth-sky sessions, **Ground** for endless mode.

## 6. The visual experience (Shambu's UI vision, sequenced honestly)

The screen is a *real* night sky, and **the objects that are sounding, glow** —
in time with their own voices. This is already wired: the audio engine exposes
per-voice levels (`getLevels()`, built in Phase 3) precisely so the star-field
can pulse each star with its own tone. Shambu's instinct and the architecture
already agree.

The full dream — traversing space AND time and playing every scene — is the
product's north star. We climb to it on the truth-preserving ladder:

1. **v1 (Phase 4):** your sky, from your place/time — accurate, calm, alive;
   sounding stars glow; the lead's current speaker is the brightest glow.
2. **Constellation focus:** tap/zoom a constellation → hear its shape-motif.
3. **Time traversal:** scrub nights/years/centuries (precession + proper
   motion switched ON so long spans stay true — the data already carries it).
4. **Planets & Moon:** requires an ephemeris (not in HYG); candidate:
   the MIT-licensed Astronomy Engine library — verify licence + accuracy at
   build time. Wandering planets = slow-moving guest voices.
5. **Deep sky:** galaxies/nebulae (OpenNGC, CC0) as new instruments with their
   own honest labels; **black-hole mood** as an explicitly artistic mode.
6. **Free space-time flight** — the far horizon. Honesty note: full 3D
   universe navigation is a massive engine in itself; we approach it stepwise
   and only if the earlier rungs are loved. Most of the emotional payload
   (per the references) lives in rungs 1–4.

Design tone throughout: **radical calm**. One dark screen, the sky, the sound,
almost no chrome. The comfort is the absence of demands.

## 7. The truth covenant (unchanged, restated for the grammar era)

- The grammar **chooses among what is true; it never invents what isn't
  there**. Melody = a path through the real stars. Identity = real geometry.
  Arc = the real night. Weather = real sky statistics.
- Artistic clothing (scale, instruments, tempo, time-compression, shimmer) is
  curated and **labeled** as artistic. The UI keeps saying so plainly.
- Determinism everywhere: same place + moment + config → the identical piece,
  forever. (Birth-sky keepsakes depend on it.)
- Every pitch on-scale, provably — the quantization guarantee extends to the
  lead, the motifs, and every development transform.

## 8. How we'll know it works (validation, absorbed from the brainstorm)

Ear gates remain supreme (Shambu judges). Structured listening tests:
- **Recognition:** the same constellation from different places/dates — does
  it feel like the same character?
- **Contrast:** two different skies blind — do they feel like different
  weather from one world?
- **Repetition tolerance:** 10+ minutes on loop — still pleasant? (the
  handpan bar)
- **The arc test:** does the bloom land — do you *feel* the culmination?
- **The gesture test:** does one-star-then-the-universe give the lump in the
  throat we're aiming for?

Plus the automated invariants: determinism, on-scale purity, note budget
respected, silence budget respected, voice-leading distance bounded.

## ADDENDUM (2026-08-08) — §6b THE FORM LAYER: the night as a setlist
*Added after the A3 full-length listen ("still lacks rhythms, patterns, building
up, transitioning"). Ratified by Shambu at the strategy checkpoint.*

Evolution is not form. A3 gave us continuous drift; music needs **articulated
sections, smoothly joined** — changes you clearly hear, executed gracefully.
Corrections to our own earlier rules:

- **Repetition belongs at the MESO scale** — a pattern must repeat enough to be
  *learned*, or it never becomes a groove. **Non-repetition belongs at the MACRO
  scale** — the piece never returns to the same state. A3 had this inverted
  (incommensurate everything = no groove can form).
- **MOVEMENTS:** the session timeline partitions into scenes of ~60–120 s, each
  anchored to a real salient structure the engine already detects (the Cygnus
  movement, the still deep-night movement, the bloom movement). Each movement
  has a distinct meso identity — its own figuration pattern, register, density,
  rhythmic feel. Contrast BETWEEN movements, repetition WITHIN them.
- **MOTIF AS OSTINATO:** during a constellation's movement its shape-motif is
  the *repeating groove* of the section, not a one-shot chime. Identity, rhythm
  and truth fuse.
- **TRANSITIONS ARE FIRST-CLASS OBJECTS** (the DJ-mix model, Shambu's framing):
  10–15 s zones where the outgoing pattern thins as the incoming establishes,
  with a breath or fill at the seam. Noticeable, never abrupt; gradual, never
  imperceptible.
- **THE PATTERN-VOCABULARY TRUTH BOUNDARY:** the sky decides *which* patterns,
  *when*, *how dense*, *in what harmony*, *toward what climax*; the pattern
  vocabulary itself is composed clothing — same covenant category as timbre and
  tempo, labeled as such. Star positions are not asked to invent rhythm; they
  conduct it.

Process rule, learned the hard way: **form is iterated in HQ sketches first**
(score → HQ renderer → Shambu's ear, hours per loop) and engine-ized only after
an approved sketch exists. No form code before a felt sketch.
