# Cosmophony — the v1 product walkthrough (the experience contract)
*Ratified by Shambu 2026-08-08. Every build decision serves THIS experience.*

## The one principle
**The camera is your eyes. The clock and the place are the conductor.**
Music changes only when WHEN or WHERE changes (or the mood lens). Pan, pinch,
zoom, scroll change what you SEE, never what you hear. Exploration is visual;
music follows the sky's own time.

## The core scenario (80% of usage — "Tonight" / ambient mode)
Open app → no login, no setup → it knows tonight + here (geolocation optional,
city fallback) → your real sky fades in → ONE button → within a second the sky
sings: GROUND drone (space), CHORD (the bright stars up now, sustained),
FIGURATION weave (handpan-like motion through the same true notes), LEAD (a
star speaks when it really rises/culminates/sets — and GLOWS as it sounds).
Put the phone down; it plays on, slowly turning with the real sky. Endless,
never repeating exactly.

## The keepsake (20% — "Birth Sky")
Date + optional time + city → an ~11-minute COMPOSED piece: opening anchor
star alone → movements anchored to that night's real structures → the bloom at
the night's true climax → return to the opening star. Plus a shareable
clip/poster ("made with <app> · url"). The gift moment.

## v1 surface (ALL of it)
One screen. Two modes (Tonight / Birth Sky). Five mood lenses (Aurora /
Embrace / Sonata / Pulse / Ground). Play-pause. Pan/zoom starfield (visual
only). Glow-sync. The honesty label. Nothing else.

## Explicitly NOT v1 (roadmap rungs, in order)
Constellation tap-focus → device-motion sky (Google-Sky style) → planets/Moon
(ephemeris) → century time-scrub (precession+proper motion ON) → deep-sky/
galaxies (OpenNGC) + black-hole artistic mode → free space-time flight.
Zoom NEVER changes music in any version; tap-focus is a deliberate act.

## Numbers (grounded)
8,849 naked-eye stars (from 119,626 catalogue rows) · ~1,500 cities · 89
constellations (~20 motif-capable) · scale of 5–7 notes per session chosen by
sky weather (never all 12 — consonance by construction) · full 11-min score
computes in ~1 s on-device · ~800 KB data · offline, private, no accounts.

## Instrument logic (Slice B)
Instrument = MOOD LENS (user's choice) × ROLE (ground/chord/figuration/lead/
weather), shaded by the star's real colour (B–V). Not per-object. Patterns =
composed vocabulary; the sky conducts (which/when/density/harmony/climax).

## Ship path
Music (A4 movements → B instruments+mix-law) → Phase 4 crisp starfield UI →
Phase 5 PWA + share export → Capacitor wrap → App Store. Light, fast, private,
airplane-mode-capable.
